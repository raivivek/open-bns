@TITLE THE BHARATIYA NYAYA SANHITA, 2023
@ACTNO NO. 45 OF 2023
@DATE [25th December, 2023.]
P0| An Act to consolidate and amend the provisions relating to offences and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto.
P1| BE it enacted by Parliament in the Seventy-fourth Year of the Republic of India as follows:––
@CHAPTER I
@CHAPTER-TITLE PRELIMINARY
@SECTION 1 | Short title, commencement and application.
P1| (1) This Act may be called the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023.
P1| (2) It shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, appoint, and different dates may be appointed for different provisions of this Sanhita.
